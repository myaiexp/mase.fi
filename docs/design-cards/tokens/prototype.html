Surfaces

Elevation ramp

Three app surfaces over true-black, plus a shared hover tint and two overlay surfaces. The steps are close on purpose — elevation is structural (borders), not shadow. See the elevation card for the overlay contract.

Borders

1px dividers

No drop shadows anywhere. Overlays escalate to the stronger rim so they don't bleed into the black.

Foreground

Text hierarchy

--fg-* is canonical; --text* are live back-compat aliases pointing at the same value. Prefer --fg-* in new code.
