Rejections

What not to do

Each banned thing rendered next to the right answer — the contrast is the point.

❌ emoji
🔔 →
— breaks color, scale, weight. Not on the table.
❌ filled
→
— solid mass overpowers stroked UI.
❌ cog
→
— cog is too detailed at 14px. Use sliders-horizontal.
❌ hardcoded
→
— stroke must be currentColor; the parent decides hue.
❌ bespoke
→
— no custom drawings. Pick the closest Lucide metaphor.
