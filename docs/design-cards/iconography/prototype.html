Iconography

One family. One weight. Line icons at text weight — currentColor · fill:none · 1.5 stroke · round caps, on a 20- or 24-grid. Every glyph below is a raw <svg class="icon"> driven entirely by base.css.

Sizes

Few fixed steps

.icon is 14px (inline, sidebar, table cells). .icon-16 steps up for toolbars and icon-only buttons. helm's sidebar runs 20px via --icon-size. That is the whole scale — no arbitrary sizes in between.

allowed 14 · .icon 16 · .icon-16 20 · helm
— banned — 12 · too small 18 · arbitrary 24 · too big — if a slot needs an off-scale size, the slot is wrong, not the size.
Weight

1.5 stroke, round caps

2 over-prints at small sizes. 1 vanishes on dense rows. Butt caps and miter joins harden the corners. 1.5 + round is the fleet weight.

right 1.5 · round
— banned — 1 · thin 2 · heavy butt · miter
One family, two grids

20-grid and 24-grid read as one hand

mase.fi draws with Lucide (24-grid); helm ships a hand-drawn 20-grid set. Both keep stroke-width: 1.5 + round caps, so at the same rendered size they land within a hair of each other — 6.25% vs 7.5% optical stroke. A 16-grid at 1.5 (9.4%) would read visibly heavier — that's the one grid to avoid.

In context · real base.css classes

Alignment falls out of the class

Sidebar rows and buttons are flex — the parent centers, the icon just drops in. Table cells and labels are inline text — .icon's vertical-align centers it on the baseline. No vertical-align:-2px hacks anywhere below.

Color

currentColor · no exceptions

Same icon, eight contexts. The icon never carries a color of its own — the surrounding text role does, so it re-tints under any theme for free.

on text --fg-1 --fg-2 --fg-3
on accent --accent --green --red --cyan --purple
Rejections

What not to do

Each banned thing rendered next to the right answer — the contrast is the point.

❌ emoji
🔔→
— breaks color, scale, weight at once. Never on the table.
❌ filled
→
— a solid mass overpowers stroked UI. (A tiny node-dot is the only fill allowed.)
❌ cog
→
— cog is too detailed small. Use sliders-horizontal.
❌ hardcoded
→
— stroke must be currentColor; the parent decides hue.
❌ bespoke
→
— no one-off drawings. Pick the closest Lucide metaphor.
