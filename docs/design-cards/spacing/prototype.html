spacing

Six steps, one per rung of the containment tree. Deeper nesting → tighter gap. Use tokens and .gap-*, never raw px or margin on stack children.

The relationship ladder

One step per rung

The step isn't chosen by eye — it's chosen by how deeply two things belong together. The anchor column is where the fleet already ships that step (buttons, inputs, modal).

--space-12px Intra-row seam — two lines of one object: card title → its metadata · tightest legible gap --space-24px Intra-control / field seam — icon→label, dot→text, label→input, input→helper · .btn gap · .form-label · .helper --space-38px Control padding · dense rows — inner padding of a control; gap between homogeneous list rows · .btn · .input padding --space-412px Between fields · section padding — distinct labeled fields; padding inside a panel · form composition --space-516px Pane padding · between sections — a pane's own padding; grouped sections on one surface · .modal padding --space-624px Between landmarks · page gutter — top-level regions; the outermost gutter · page composition

The subtle one

Rows sit at 8px, fields at 12px

Same-looking vertical stacks, different rung. Homogeneous rows only need a seam; heterogeneous labeled fields need clear grouping — the 4px internal seam demands a 3× jump between fields so nothing reads as orphaned.

Rules in action

Right · cramped · loose

The same three-field form at the correct rung, then broken two ways — everything jammed to the 4px seam, then everything blown out to the 24px landmark step. Both destroy the grouping the ramp exists to create.

The stacking rule

gap on the parent, not margin on children

Rhythm belongs to the container. .gap-* now covers all six rungs, so there's no stack a parent can't own — margins are the intra-field seams only.
